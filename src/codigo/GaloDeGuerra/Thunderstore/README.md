# War Rooster

Client-side only. Both fighters need this mod.

Press F8 to challenge another player to a 1v1. You bet an item from the bag. Coins, gems, a weapon, armor or another item all work. The bet leaves the bag and does not stay on the body.

The button can turn PvP on for both. Whoever falls to the other loses. A fall, a creature or hunger do not count. Forfeit hands the bet over. At the end both see what the winner took.

If the bag is full, make a free slot and the prize goes in.
