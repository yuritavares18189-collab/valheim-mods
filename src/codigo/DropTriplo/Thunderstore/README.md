# Triple Drop

Client-side only. Only your own game needs this mod.

What you pick up comes in three times. Items from your own death stay at the normal amount: the grave, and whatever fell where you died. Your own chest, cart and bag drops stay normal too. The forge still spends the normal amount.

Extra copies stop when your carry weight is full. Weightless items still come in until the bag has no free slot.

Open Configuration Manager with F1. Times is how many copies come in. The default is 3.
